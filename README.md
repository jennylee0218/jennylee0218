**Natural-language analytics assistant (BCG)**: lets executives query semi-structured business data in plain English, with SQL validation and access-control guardrails between the LLM and the data.

**ETF market microstructure research (Jane Street)**: presented to senior analysts across the quant trading and institutional sales teams.
